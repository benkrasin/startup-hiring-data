FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Only from the public job-board APIs that Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby provide so companies' jobs can be listed elsewhere. No scraping of websites, no personal data. It holds job postings only.

How fresh is it?

It's a snapshot taken on 2026-10-14. Some roles close every day, so treat it as a point-in-time view and click through to apply links. Monthly refreshed editions are planned.
