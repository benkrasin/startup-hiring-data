Who is hiring for AI

2,307 titles mention AI, ML, LLM, inference or research science. The most come from OpenAI (132), Anthropic (124), SpaceX (100), Waymo (89), Databricks (74), Scale AI (47), Snowflake (45) and Cerebras (40). "Forward deployed" is now a real job family: 315 open postings carry that title.

Seniority skew

14,260 titles (44%) say senior, staff, principal, lead, head or director. Only 1,647 (5%) say intern, junior, new grad, entry or associate.

Method

Only the public job-board APIs that ATS vendors provide for syndicating postings; no website scraping, no personal data. Remote = the posting's work-type field says remote (or, if empty, the location says remote and not hybrid). Title matching is regex-based and approximate. This is a point-in-time snapshot.
